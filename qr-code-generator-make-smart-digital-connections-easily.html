What Does QR Code Mean?

QR code is a two-dimensional digital barcode which stores digital information and provides instant access to information by scanning the code using a mobile device.
In contrast to the usual barcodes, QR codes are able to store more information and provide access to websites, payments, digital menu, and other things online. Like we see,Brands insert link to QR code s for packaging of their products in order to provide additional information, instructions, reviews, or promotions.Restaurants use QR codes for digital menus and allow customers to have access to the menu via smartphone.

Different Types of QR Codes

QR codes can be generated in several ways according to the type of information required by the user to be shared through them.There are mainly two types Static QR codes and dynamic QR codes as they help users link to digital information in a simple and efficient way.

Static QR Codes

Static QR codes store data or information within the QR code which cannot be updated once it is generated as the data is fixed in these codes. These QR codes are easy-to-use and reliable for storing permanent information like website link, contact information, message or other business-related data.
For example, a restaurant can use static QR codes which direct customers to visit their fixed website or menu card online. Similarly, any company can use static QR codes on its business cards, packaging, or any other promotional material.

Dynamic QR Codes

Dynamic QR codes are highly flexible since the destination data may be changed after the QR code creation. They are applicable to companies that need to update their content, campaign, or promotional message.
For example, a restaurant can use the same QR code at its tables and change the menu connected to the QR code in case of introduction of new products or price changes. In addition, the use of dynamic QR codes is suitable for seasonal promotions, event registration, and marketing campaigns.

QR Code Creation Best Practices

QR code creation is an easy process, but best practices help to make sure that users have no trouble in scanning and obtaining information.

  • Make Use of the Relevant Information

    Before QR code generator free, consider which information is needed. QR code should take users to a place that is valuable for them like a page of a website or some useful resources.

  • Use Appropriate Size

    QR code should be of appropriate size and situated in a way so users can easily scan it. Too small QR code or incorrect positioning of it can prevent successful scanning.

  • Optimize Destination Page

    While generating QR code for a webpage, make sure that webpage is functional on mobile devices because people use mostly smartphones to scan QR codes.

  • Provide Additional Context about the QR Code

    The person needs to be aware of what he or she would get upon scanning the QR code. An instruction like “Scan to visit our website” can promote user engagement.

  • Do Your Testing First

    It is crucial to scan the QR code several times from different devices prior to publishing. This will help detect any possible technical difficulties.
