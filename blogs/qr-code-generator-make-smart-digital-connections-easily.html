What Does QR Code Mean?

QR code is a two-dimensional digital barcode which stores digital information and provides instant access to information by scanning the code using a mobile device.
In contrast to the usual barcodes, QR codes are able to store more information and provide access to websites, payments, digital menu, and other things online. Like we see,Brands insert link to QR code s for packaging of their products in order to provide additional information, instructions, reviews, or promotions.Restaurants use QR codes for digital menus and allow customers to have access to the menu via smartphone.

Different Types of QR Codes

There are mainly two types of QR codes: Static QR codes and Dynamic QR codes.

Static QR Codes

Static QR codes contain fixed information that cannot be changed after creation. They are useful for permanent information such as website links, contact details, and business information.

Dynamic QR Codes

Dynamic QR codes allow users to update the destination information after creation. They are useful for campaigns, promotions, and frequently changing content.
